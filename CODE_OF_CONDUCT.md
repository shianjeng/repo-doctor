# Code of conduct

Repo Doctor is a small project that wants to be a friendly place for everyone, whatever their experience, background, or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and assume good intent. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Help newcomers. Everyone’s first pull request was once somebody’s first.
- Accept that maintainers may say no, and explain your reasoning when you do.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, personal attacks, sharing someone’s private information, and sustained disruption of discussions are not welcome in issues, pull requests, or any other project space.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer privately through the contact route on the [maintainer’s GitHub profile](https://github.com/shianjeng). Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, and may block people who break this code of conduct.

This policy is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
